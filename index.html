HOW TO PLAY

  • Trap, then pop. Blow a bubble over a bot to trap it, then touch the bubble to burst it. Pop several at once for 1,000 · 2,000 · 4,000 · 8,000 · 16,000…
  • Move ◀ ▶ / arrows / A D. BUBBLE = Space or Z. JUMP = ▲ / W / X.
  • Ride a bubble: hold JUMP as you land on one and you stand on it instead of bursting it — that is how you reach the high ledges.
  • Fall through the floor shafts and you come back in at the top.
  • E-X-T-E-N-D: pop three or more bots at once and letter bubbles drift up the shafts. Collect all six for an extra bot and a free round.
  • Special bubbles: water floods, lightning cuts across, fire spreads along a platform. Only a pop of YOURS sets one off.
  • Take too long and it is HURRY UP — the bots turn angry, then the invincible AUDITOR comes for you. Clear fast and the round's food is worth more (up to 7,000).
  • 3 bots. Extra ones at 30,000 · 100,000 · 400,000. 100 rounds, and the Foreman waits at the end.
